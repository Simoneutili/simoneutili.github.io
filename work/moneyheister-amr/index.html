RSA Hackathon · TU Delft · Sep 2025

A Package-Carrying Robot for a Competitive Arena

My first hackathon: a remote-controlled robot that collects, protects, and delivers packages

Simone Utili & Team

Code

The competition had a fixed goal: build a remote-controlled mobile robot that carries packages from a dropper to a receiver while protecting them from rival robots on the arena floor.

  • 3D-Printed Gear Lift
  • Laser-Cut Chassis
  • Differential Drive
  • Rapid Prototyping

The design

We built a laser-cut, differential-drive chassis around an internal lift made of 3D-printed gears. The package stays enclosed inside the robot while it drives and is then raised up to the receiver. A horizontal arm on the outside keeps opposing robots at a distance.

What I took from it

This was my first hackathon, and it stays close to my heart. Beyond designing the gears and wiring the drive base, it is where I first learned to work against the clock in a team and deliver a working robot by a hard deadline.
