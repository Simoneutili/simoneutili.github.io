Online Real2Sim Calibration of Velocity-Controlled Digital Twins via Reinforcement Learning

Simone Utili, Andrea Govoni, Gianluca Palli

Online curriculum reinforcement learning framework in NVIDIA Isaac Lab that calibrates PhysX joint damping and friction parameters of a velocity-controlled 7-DoF PAL TIAGo digital twin from real-time velocity residuals.

First author · Developed from BSc thesis research at the Laboratory of Automation and Robotics (LAR), University of Bologna.
